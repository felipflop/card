# 2026-11-21: Coil generator and first footprint

Wrote `tools/coil_gen.py`. It sweeps turn counts for a rectangular spiral that follows the card outline and picks the largest inductance that still resonates at or above 13.56 MHz, since the trim cap and the lamination can only pull the frequency down.

With a 1.2 mm margin to the edge, 0.3 mm traces and 0.3 mm gaps, it picked 6 turns:

```
picked N=6: L=6.57 uH, Rdc=2.53 ohm, f0=14.06 MHz, trim cap to hit 13.56 MHz = 1.5 pF
```

The footprint (`hardware/libs/card.pretty/NFC_Coil_83x52_N6.kicad_mod`) has the mask opened over the copper so the gold coil shows on the front, and both pads are a net tie so DRC doesn't complain that the coil shorts LA to LB.

Also moved the Python tooling to uv. The venv uses system site packages so it can see KiCad's `pcbnew` and `wx`, which KiBot needs. `make check` prints pcbnew 10.0.8 and KiBot 1.9.1.

## Later correction

The generator assumed 17 pF for the tag. The NTAG X DNA datasheet (Rev 3.2) gives 18.4 pF typical, which puts this coil at 13.58 MHz untuned, with no margin for lamination. See [first principles §2](../first-principles.md#2-the-antenna-is-an-lc-tank). The coil needs regenerating for about 6.2 µH.
