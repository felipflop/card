Six ways to put the shortlisted features together. The front is matte mask with exposed ENIG gold and the coil; the back is the laser-engraved polycarbonate skin. Press Tap to see what a phone does to each one. Every field below edits all six cards at once, including the LED colour.

These are mockups drawn to the card's real proportions (85.6 × 54 mm, 3.18 mm corners) and the real coil footprint: six 0.3 mm turns at 0.6 mm pitch. Colours, glow and engraving contrast are simulated; the real red will be softer through the skin, and the starfield and polarized image depend on materials I haven't sampled yet.
