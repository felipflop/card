Avant-garde

My pick: build A on every card as the baseline, and put one avant-garde option on a few prototype-1 cards. E (edge-lit engraving) and F (reflector pinholes) look the most striking for the least process risk, and both keep the gold front untouched. Both reuse what the plan already has: the laser-engraved PC skin and the clear epoxy pocket fill.

Why not reverse-mount: the parts are 1.05 mm thick and the lens has to pass through a hole. On 0.11 mm flex it would stand about 0.9 mm proud of the front.

1.8 V caveat for all options: hyper-red AlGaInP sits near 1.7 V at 1 mA, so the LEDs run at a few hundred µA with no series resistors, and brightness comes from PWM. I'll pick the LED by its I–V curve at 0.5 mA, not by its 20 mA rating.
